These 33 original PNG files are copied byte-for-byte without recoloring, tracing, cropping, drawing or generation. Forge selects the recorded pose and scales it only for display. Per-file source links, SHA-256 hashes, dimensions, exact catalog IDs and match-review rationale are in provenance.json. No upstream technique prose is reused.

Forge 0.6.0 includes 11 full-frame original PNG exports from the same pinned repository. Upstream filenames ending in `.ai.png` are historical Adobe Illustrator exports, not newly generated artwork. Six are activity references (boxing, cycling, stationary cycling, running, swimming and walking), not complete form tutorials.
